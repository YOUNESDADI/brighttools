Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy describes the current BrightTools website. It must be updated if we add analytics, advertising, accounts, forms, or other services that change how information is handled.

Information you enter into tools

In this version, image compression and resizing, word counting, case conversion, and password generation run in your browser. BrightTools does not intentionally upload the contents you enter into those tools to its own application server. Your browser, device, extensions, or other software may still process or store information according to their own settings.

QR code generator and third parties

The QR code generator loads the QRCode.js library from a third-party CDN. When your browser requests that library, the CDN may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, request time, and browser-related headers under its own policies. Avoid entering confidential or sensitive information into a QR code.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is currently published through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may collect technical information in server logs and process data as described in its own policies. Review GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

BrightTools currently has no intentional first-party analytics or advertising scripts installed. This may change in the future. If advertising or analytics are added, third-party providers may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, or similar technologies subject to their policies and applicable law. This policy must be updated before those services are activated, and any required consent mechanism must be implemented.

Children and sensitive information

BrightTools is a general-purpose utility website, not designed to collect personal information. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through public issue reports or encode it into a QR code that others can access.

Changes and contact

This policy may be revised when the website changes. Check this page for the latest version. For questions or privacy concerns, visit the Contact page.

This is a practical starting policy, not legal advice. Review it for the laws applicable to your audience and update it to reflect the site's actual services.
